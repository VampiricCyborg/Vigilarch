# Ops console (M5)

React + TypeScript. The distinctive part is not the dashboard; it is the coverage-aware
component library.

No aggregate may reach this UI without a coverage annotation, and coverage must not be
suppressible (§14 M5 — enforce with a type-level or lint rule). A trend line spanning
periods of 94%% and 61%% coverage is drawn with a widening uncertainty band, and the
console refuses to state a direction when the coverage delta exceeds the apparent effect
size.

A dashboard that cannot say "I do not know" will eventually say something false about
whether people are getting hurt.
