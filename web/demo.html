Vigilarch — sealing, interactively

Two nodes keep their own hash chains while disconnected. When they meet they co-sign each other's current chain head, and that signature is what bounds a record's creation time — no later than the meeting. A record with such an attestation over it is sealed; a record without one is unwitnessed, and the honest answer there is a wide open window, never a guess.

Append to either chain, exchange attestations, then click any entry to see its bracket. Equivocate to watch one key sign two irreconcilable entries and get convicted — while the withheld sibling stays unsealed. This runs the real vigil-ledger compiled to wasm32: the same chain, DAG, bracket and fork-detection code vigil-sim and vigil-node run natively.
